# Weekly Planner

The weekly planner gives you a week-focused view of scheduled activities, helping you understand what's coming up and how your time is arranged.

## See the week ahead

Use the weekly view to review scheduled activities across the days of the week. Looking at several days together can help you spot busy periods and make realistic plans.

## Plan your schedule

When adding or arranging an activity, use clear titles and choose the relevant date and time if those fields are available. Leave enough space between activities for transitions, breaks, and unexpected changes.

## Work with tasks and categories

Tasks and scheduled activities serve related but different purposes: a task represents work to do, while a scheduled item indicates when you intend to do it. Use categories, where available, to keep different areas of life organized.

## Build a realistic week

- Start with fixed commitments.
- Add deadlines and important responsibilities.
- Break larger tasks into manageable work sessions.
- Leave room for breaks and changes.
- Review the plan regularly and adjust when needed.

## Review and adjust

A weekly plan is a guide, not a rigid contract. If plans change, update the schedule to reflect what is realistic now.
